Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often.

How do I add my first expense?

Go to Expenses in the sidebar and click Add Expense. Enter the amount, pick a category, add an optional note and payment method, then choose a date. Hit Save and it will instantly appear in your ledger, dashboard, and reports.

How do I create a monthly budget?

Open the Budgets page, click Add Budget, select a category, set your monthly limit, and save. Spendly will track your usage and alert you when you approach or exceed your limit.

Can I set savings goals?

Absolutely! Visit the Goals page and create a goal with a target amount and deadline. You can add funds manually as you save, and Spendly will show your progress with a progress bar.

How do I export my financial data?

Go to Settings → Export & Backup. You can download your transactions as a CSV or create a full backup of all your Spendly data. Your privacy matters — your data is yours, always.

How do I enable two-factor authentication?

Head to Settings → Security and toggle on Two-Factor Authentication. Each time you sign in you'll enter a one-time code from your authenticator app for an extra layer of protection.

Can I change my currency or language?

Yes — open Settings → Preferences to change your currency, date format, language, and week start day. These preferences apply across the entire app instantly.

How do I switch between light and dark mode?

Use the theme switcher in the top navigation bar. You can pick Light, Dark, or System (which follows your device's preference). Your choice is saved automatically.

Is my financial data secure and private?

Yes. Spendly uses secure, hashed passwords, and your data is never sold or shared with third parties. You control everything under Settings → Data & Privacy. See our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

Common Troubleshooting

Simple fixes for issues you might run into.

Page not loading

Try refreshing the page, clearing your browser cache, or disabling browser extensions that may block the app.

Data not saving

Check your internet connection and make sure you're signed in. Log out and back in, then try saving again.

Mobile layout issues

Ensure your browser is up to date. On phones, landscape mode gives the most space for the dashboard and tables.

Can't sign in

Double-check your email and password, or use the "Forgot password" link to reset your credentials securely.
